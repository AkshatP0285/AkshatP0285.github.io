## About Me

I'm a researcher and data scientist specializing in **medical AI**, **deep learning**, and **large language model orchestration**. Currently, I'm a Research Associate at **IIT Jodhpur** under Prof. Deepak Mishra, where I develop intelligent systems for automated rib fracture detection and clinical decision support.

My research interests span:
- **Medical Imaging**: Building clinically interpretable deep learning pipelines for CT/imaging analysis
- **Neuro-Symbolic AI**: Combining neural networks with symbolic reasoning for transparent decision-making
- **LLM Systems**: Multi-agent architectures, RAG, and LLM-as-reasoning modules in clinical workflows
- **Biomedical ML**: FAIR-compliant reproducible models for healthcare applications

### Key Research Areas:
- Rib fracture detection, localization, and treatment outcome prediction
- Mistake-guided hierarchy learning for robust classification
- Multi-modal learning with medical text and imaging
- Forensic AI applications

I'm passionate about building **reproducible, clinically-grounded AI systems** that bridge the gap between cutting-edge research and practical healthcare applications.
